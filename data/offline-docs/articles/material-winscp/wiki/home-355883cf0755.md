# WinSCP Material

WinSCP Material is the Windows Electron port of WinSCP with a Material Design
3 interface, SFTP/SCP/FTP/FTPS/WebDAV/S3/local adapters, a transfer queue, and
the project's bilingual language and appearance requirements.

## Verified handoff

The current default-branch tip is
[`50233a09522f551ed1c7c4e43f47af8cf0c8c63c`](https://github.com/Ding-Ding-Projects/material-winscp/commit/50233a09522f551ed1c7c4e43f47af8cf0c8c63c).
The smoke-tested state is **3,190 tests, 3,189 passing, 0 failing, 1 skipped**.

This handoff wires real session, protocol, UI, synchronization, notification,
resource, preference, and foreground-transfer seams. It adds the live
`transfer.cancel(sessionId)` bridge, invalidates late watcher comparisons,
refreshes remote panels from the configured interval, and hardens SFTP policy
validation. Focused and real Electron/SFTP smoke tests cover the changes.
