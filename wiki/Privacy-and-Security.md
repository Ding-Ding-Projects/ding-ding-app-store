# Privacy and security

The App Store retains only necessary app-managed records, explains requests for paths/network/privileges, and keeps sensitive values in OS credential storage.

- Configuration: inspect/export records, choose retention where available, adjust local settings.
- Failure: storage, permission, config, network, export, and history errors describe the real condition without leaks.
- Security: validate privileged inputs, isolate source builds, redact logs, and preserve encryption in history.
- Verification: integration and threat-model/runtime testing remain pending.

Detailed source: `docs/features/privacy-and-security.md`.
